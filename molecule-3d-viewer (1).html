Structures and 3D conformers are retrieved live from PubChem (National Center for Biotechnology Information). Force classifications are generated with simplified educational heuristics based on molecular properties (polarity, H-bond donor/acceptor counts, elemental composition) — they illustrate the dominant forces typically taught at introductory level, not a rigorous computational analysis. Single elements are shown as isolated atoms with their characteristic bulk-bonding behavior noted, since a lone atom cannot itself exhibit intermolecular forces.
